About the Band

We are an all-round pop/rock cover band from Limburg, Belgium.

We all share a big passion for music, both old and new, and love to make people happy by playing their favorite songs.

Our repertoire spans several decades, combining timeless classics with modern hits. Whether it's a festival, wedding, private party, pub, or corporate event, we enjoy creating an energetic atmosphere that gets people singing and dancing.
